# ADR-NNNN: Title (imperative, e.g. "Adopt X for Y")

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR-NNNN | Rejected
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** GitHub handles of maintainers who approved
- **Security impact:** None | Low | Moderate | High (High requires two maintainer approvals and a threat note)

## Context

What situation forces a decision? What constraints apply (platform, Swift, security, API-stability)? Two or three paragraphs, written for a contributor reading this in five years with no other context.

## Decision

The decision, stated in one or two declarative sentences. Then the design as adopted, in enough detail that an implementation matching this ADR is unambiguous.

## Alternatives Considered

For each serious alternative: what it was, and the specific reason it lost. "We didn't like it" is not a reason; "it makes the insecure path shorter than the secure one" is.

## Consequences

- What becomes easier.
- What becomes harder or is given up.
- Migration/compatibility implications.
- **Security consequences** — always present, even if "none identified"; state what an attacker gains or loses under this decision.

## Revisit When

Concrete conditions under which this decision should be re-examined (e.g. "if Apple ships a first-party X", "if the platform floor reaches iOS N").
